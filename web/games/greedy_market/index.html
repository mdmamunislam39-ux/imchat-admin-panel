HOW TO PLAY

1. Select your desired chip value from the bottom tray.

2. Tap any of the 8 fruit/food items to place your bet during the 30-second betting phase.

3. When the timer hits zero, betting closes and the roulette wheel will spin.

4. If the highlight stops on an item you bet on, you win your bet multiplied by that item's multiplier (5x, 10x, 15x, 25x, or 45x)!

5. Prize Pool Bonus: The prize pool increases as more players participate. If Fruit/Salad is opened, all players who chose fruit win. If Pizza is opened, all players who chose meat win!

6. The daily round number and Top 10 winners list reset every day at 10:00 PM.

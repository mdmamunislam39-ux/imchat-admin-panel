Salad Rules

How to play

1

Betting Period

Each round lasts 30 seconds. During the countdown, you can select your chip amount and place bets on any food items.

2

Wheel Spinning

When the 30s timer reaches 0, betting is locked. The golden spotlight rapidly revolves around the 8 items and stops on the winning food.

3

Winning Payouts

Players betting on the winning food receive their bet multiplied by the food's multiplier:

🌭 Hot Dog: 10x
🍢 BBQ Skewer: 15x
🍖 Ham Leg: 25x
🥩 Steak: 45x
🥕 Carrot: 5x
🌽 Corn: 5x
🥬 Cabbage: 5x
🍅 Tomato: 5x
4

Salad & Pizza Specials

When the wheel stops on Salad, all 4 vegetable items (5x) win simultaneously! When the wheel stops on Pizza, all 4 meat items (10x, 15x, 25x, 45x) win simultaneously!

5

Today's Prize Pool

A portion of every bet enters the Today's Prize pool, awarded to players on lucky jackpot rounds!

6

Regular & Advanced Chips

Switch between Regular (5, 100, 500, 1000) and Advanced (2k, 5k, 10k, 50k) chip modes to fit your playing style.

7

Diamonds & Coins

Use yellow or black diamonds to redeem game coins and play anytime. Collect daily winnings to rise up the leaderboard!
